## problem link
https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/467/A

## approach
inputting the data and increasing count by 1 for every room that had less than 1 occupants of its total occupacy

## code
````python
n = int(input())

rooms = [list(map(int, input().split())) for i in range(n)]

count=0
for i in range(n):
    if rooms[i][1]-rooms[i][0]>1:
        count=count+1

print(count)        
````
